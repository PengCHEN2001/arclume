# Payment Problems and Apparent Duplicate Charges

## Pending versus completed payments

Two entries on a bank statement do not by themselves prove that a customer
was charged twice. One entry may be a pending authorization while another is
a completed payment. The customer should check the transaction status and
dates with their payment provider before assuming both were settled.

## What support can do

If two completed charges appear for one order, the customer can contact
billing support with the order number, the two dates, the amounts, and a
masked payment reference. Support must not ask for a full card number or
password.

## Verification boundary

This article explains the next steps; it cannot access payment records,
confirm that a duplicate charge occurred, or issue a refund. A refund request
is handled under the separate Refund Policy.
