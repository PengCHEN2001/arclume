# Arclume Mini Desk Lamp (AL-M100)

## Product overview

The Mini is a compact desk lamp for reading and small workspaces. Model AL-M100
has three white-light color settings and touch controls for brightness. It is
powered through a USB-C cable connected to a 5 V power source. The package
contains the lamp and cable; a wall adapter is not included.

## Connectivity and limitations

The Mini has no Wi-Fi radio and does not connect to the Arclume mobile app. It
cannot use app schedules or remote control. Touch controls on the lamp are the
only way to change its settings. If a customer asks for app control, check the
Pro model instead; do not describe the Mini as a connected lamp.
