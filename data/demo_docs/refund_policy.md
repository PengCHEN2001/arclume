# Refund Policy

Customers can request a no-questions-asked refund within seven days of purchase.
After a refund request is submitted, review usually takes one to three business
days. Once approved, the payment is returned to the original payment account in
five to seven business days.

If an order has already shipped, the customer must complete the return process
before the refund can be issued. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless
the product has a confirmed quality problem.
