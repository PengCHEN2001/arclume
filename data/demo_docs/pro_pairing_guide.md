# Pairing the Arclume Pro Desk Lamp

## Before pairing

Confirm that the lamp is model AL-P200, powered on, and close to the router.
Connect the phone to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. A 5 GHz-only network will not
pair with the Pro. The Mini model AL-M100 cannot be paired with the app.

## Pairing steps

Open the Arclume mobile app, sign in, choose Add device, and select Pro Desk
Lamp. Follow the in-app instructions to put the lamp in pairing mode and enter
the 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi password. If the lamp is not found, restart the lamp and
phone, then retry near the router. Do not ask a customer to send a Wi-Fi
password to support.

## Different failure: app sign-in

A 401 error during app sign-in is an account authentication problem, not proof
that the lamp or Wi-Fi is faulty. Check the account credentials or use the
password-reset flow before retrying pairing. If sign-in still fails, contact
technical support without sharing the password.
