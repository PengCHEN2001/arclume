# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping normally takes three to five business days and is free for
orders of at least 99 units of currency. Express shipping normally takes one to
two business days and costs 15 units of currency. Same-city delivery may arrive
the same day or the next day and costs 10 units of currency.

Remote areas may require two to three additional days. Shipping information can
take up to 24 hours to appear after an order is dispatched. Address changes are
only possible before the order is dispatched.
