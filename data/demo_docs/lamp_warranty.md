# Arclume Lamp Limited Warranty

## Coverage

The Mini (AL-M100) and Pro (AL-P200) lamps have a 12-month limited warranty
starting on the delivery date. It covers manufacturing defects that affect
normal use, such as a touch control that stops responding under ordinary
conditions. Support needs the model, proof of purchase, delivery date, and a
description of the fault before it can assess a claim.

## Exclusions and next steps

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or damage caused
by an unsuitable power source. Support may request troubleshooting before
considering a repair or replacement, and cannot promise approval before review.

## Warranty versus refund

The warranty is separate from the seven-day no-questions-asked refund policy.
A warranty claim is not an automatic refund, and a refund request is not an
automatic warranty claim.
