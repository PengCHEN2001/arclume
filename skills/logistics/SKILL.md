# Logistics Handling Skill

## Purpose

Handle delivery, tracking, delay, and address-change questions using verified
shipping information.

## Workflow

1. Identify whether the customer needs a general delivery policy or a specific
   shipment status.
2. Use the knowledge base for general delivery rules.
3. Ask for the order or tracking identifier before checking a specific shipment.
4. Explain the next action when a shipment is delayed or the address cannot be
   changed.

## Safety rules

- Do not invent a tracking event or delivery date.
- Do not state that an address was changed without a business-system result.
- Escalate an unresolved or exceptional delivery case when human intervention
  is required.

## Response style

Give a clear status, the relevant time window, and the next step.
